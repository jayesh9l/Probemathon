Most hackathons hand you a problem and ask for code. Probemathon flips it: you find the problem worth solving, pitch why it matters, and show the judges your solution before anyone else even sees the question. Open to anyone, from any college.

The concept

We flipped the usual hackathon format

A regular hackathon gives you the problem on day one. Probemathon makes finding a real, worthwhile problem the first challenge — because a great solution is worthless if it's solving the wrong thing.

A usual hackathon

Organizers hand every team the same problem statement. Teams race to build the best solution to a question they didn't choose.

→

Probemathon

You choose the problem — from campus life, daily life, or something bigger — and pitch both the problem and your solution as one idea.

How it works

Two rounds, from idea to pitch

Every team goes through the same two stages — first on paper, then on stage. Registration is open now, right up to the event.

01

Frame the problem, propose the fix

Pick a problem from college life, daily life, your country, or anywhere else worth solving. Build a PPT that lays out the problem clearly and proposes your solution. Submit it — no live presenting yet, this round is judged on the deck alone.

  • Register your team anytime before the submission deadline
  • Pick a real, specific problem — the sharper the framing, the better
  • Build a PPT covering the problem, its impact, and your proposed solution
  • Submit before 15 Oct 2026
  • Shortlist of 10–15 teams announced on 16 Oct 2026
PPT submission Any theme Deadline: 15 Oct 2026
02

Pitch it to the judges

Shortlisted teams present their problem and solution live in front of the judging panel. This is where you defend your idea, answer questions, and make the case for why it matters. Three teams walk away as winners.

  • Shortlisted teams pitch live on 17 Oct 2026 at SVPCET, Nagpur
  • Each team presents their problem and solution to the judging panel
  • Judges question teams on feasibility, originality, and impact
  • Top 3 teams are announced the same day
  • Winners take home prize money, a winning certificate, and goodies

Questions

Frequently asked

What kind of problem statement can I pick?
Anything you can make a genuine case for — a problem from college life, daily life, your city or country, or a broader issue you care about. The only requirement is that it's real and your solution addresses it directly.
Do I need to build a working prototype?
No. Round 1 is judged on your PPT — how well you frame the problem and how convincing your proposed solution is. Round 2 is about pitching that same idea live, not demoing a finished product.
How many teams make it to Round 2?
10 to 15 teams are shortlisted from all Round 1 submissions to move on to the live pitch round.
Do I need to be from your college to join?
No — Probemathon is open to students from any college, not just ours. Anyone can register and take part.
Is there a team size limit?
Yes — teams can be anywhere from 1 to 4 members (Solo, Duo, Trio, or Squad).
How much does it cost to register?
Registration is priced by team size: ₹50 for Solo, ₹80 for Duo, ₹120 for Trio, and ₹150 for Squad (4 members).
What do winning teams get?
The three winning teams receive a winning certificate along with goodies. Round 2 teams get an appreciation certificate, and everyone who submits a Round 1 entry gets a participation certificate.
